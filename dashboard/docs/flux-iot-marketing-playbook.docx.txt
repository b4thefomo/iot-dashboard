2. The Authority Marketing Framework
Traditional B2B marketing focuses on product features and benefits. Authority marketing focuses on demonstrating expertise that makes you the obvious choice.


The Core Principle
Give away the knowledge. Sell the implementation. When you teach people what they need to know about compliance, they trust you to help them achieve it.
	

The Authority Ladder
Move prospects up the ladder through increasingly valuable content:


1. Awareness: Blog posts, social content, SEO pages ("What is HACCP compliance?")
2. Interest: Guides, checklists, webinars ("HACCP Temperature Monitoring Complete Guide")
3. Consideration: Gap analysis, assessments, consultations ("Free Compliance Gap Analysis")
4. Decision: Pilots, proposals, case studies ("See how [Company] achieved 100% compliance")


Content That Builds Authority
* Regulation explainers written in plain English
* Inspection preparation guides by sector
* Compliance checklists and self-assessments
* Regulatory update alerts when rules change
* Case studies focused on compliance outcomes, not features
* Original research on compliance trends and gaps

Tier 1: Low-Friction Downloads (Email Only)
* HACCP Temperature Monitoring Checklist
* Inspection Preparation Quick Guide
* Temperature Log Templates
* Regulatory Quick Reference Cards
Purpose: Build email list, establish initial value.


Tier 2: Higher-Value Assets (More Fields)
* Complete HACCP Compliance Guide (50+ pages)
* Sector-Specific Compliance Handbooks
* ROI Calculator Spreadsheet
* Temperature Excursion Cost Analysis
Purpose: Qualify leads, demonstrate depth of expertise.


Tier 3: Interactive Tools (Highest Value)
* Free Compliance Gap Analysis (30-minute consultation)
* Compliance Readiness Score Calculator
* Custom ROI Assessment
* Mock Inspection Review
Purpose: Direct sales qualification, demonstrate consulting value.


Landing Page Formula
For each lead magnet, create a landing page with:
5. Headline that speaks to a specific pain point
6. Three bullet points of what they will learn/get
7. Social proof (logos, testimonials, or statistics)
8. Simple form (minimise fields for higher conversion)
9. Clear CTA button ("Get the Guide" not "Submit")

7. Thought Leadership Programme
Building personal authority amplifies company authority. The founder should be seen as the go-to expert on cold chain compliance.


LinkedIn Content Strategy


Post Types to Rotate:
* Regulatory updates: When rules change, be first to explain what it means
* Compliance mistakes: Common errors you see (anonymised)
* Behind-the-scenes: What you are building and why
* Customer wins: Compliance successes (with permission)
* Industry observations: Trends you are noticing
* Contrarian takes: Challenge conventional wisdom respectfully


Posting Cadence:
* Minimum 3x per week on LinkedIn
* Comment on 5-10 relevant posts daily to build visibility
* Share others' content with your perspective added


Speaking and PR Opportunities
* Submit to speak at food safety conferences
* Offer expert commentary for industry publications
* Guest on relevant podcasts
* Write guest articles for trade publications


Original Research
Publish one major research piece per year:
"State of Cold Chain Compliance 2026: What 500 Operators Told Us About Their Biggest Challenges"
	This generates PR coverage, backlinks, and positions you as the authority tracking the industry.

8. Campaign Templates


Campaign 1: Compliance Gap Analysis Offer
Goal: Generate qualified sales calls


Channels:
* LinkedIn posts and DMs
* Email to existing list
* Landing page with SEO


Messaging:
Do you know exactly where you stand against [FSA/MHRA/HACCP] requirements? We are offering free 30-minute compliance gap analyses this month. You will get a written report of any gaps, whether you work with us or not.
	

Campaign 2: Regulatory Update Alert
Goal: Stay top of mind, demonstrate expertise


Trigger: Any significant regulatory change


Execution:
10. Same day: LinkedIn post explaining what changed
11. Within 48 hours: Email to list with detailed breakdown
12. Within 1 week: Blog post/guide with full implications
13. Ongoing: Offer consultations to discuss impact


Campaign 3: Founding Partner Launch
Goal: Acquire first 5-10 customers with urgency


Offer:
We are onboarding five founding partners this quarter. You get 40% off for the first two years, plus direct input into our product roadmap. Limited to five companies.
	

Scarcity Elements:
* Limited number (5 spots)
* Limited time (this quarter only)
* Genuine exclusivity (roadmap input)
